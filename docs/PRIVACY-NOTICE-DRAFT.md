# Remera Church of the Nazarene — Privacy Notice Draft

> **Draft for church and DPO review; do not publish yet.** Confirm the production database provider and country/region, the DPO's public contact channel, and what happens to records after a request is handled. Confirm any required authorization for storage or transfer outside Rwanda before public launch.

## English

### Who is responsible

The data controller is **Church of the Nazarene–Remera**. The designated Data Protection Officer is **Byumvuhore Frederic**. Until a direct public email or phone number is confirmed, privacy questions may be sent through the [church contact form](/contact).

### Information the website receives

Depending on how you use the site, the church may receive your name, email address, phone number, address, date of birth, gender, membership and ministry details, visitor follow-up preferences, event responses, messages, prayer requests, and information staff enter for attendance, pastoral care, or giving records. Member account passwords are stored as password hashes.

### Why the church uses it

The church uses this information to answer messages, welcome visitors, provide pastoral care, maintain member accounts and church records, coordinate ministries and events, and record gifts handled by staff. The website does not process online payments.

### Who can see it

Member records are available to the member concerned and authorized church staff. Prayer requests are restricted to the designated pastoral team. The public website does not show a member directory or birthday list. Members aged 16 or older may opt in to share their **first name and birthday month/day** with signed-in members; this can be switched off in the member profile. Birthday details for members under 16 are not shared through the website.

Church-approved public content, such as ministry information, events, sermons, names, and photographs, may be shown publicly.

### Storage, providers, and retention — confirmation required

The website currently uses MongoDB. The production database provider, storage country/region, any transfers outside Rwanda, and applicable authorization must be confirmed and stated here before publication. The church intends to keep contact, visitor, and prayer submissions while they are being handled; the church must confirm when and how closed submissions are deleted or archived. Membership, attendance, care, and finance records may follow separate retention requirements.

### Your choices and requests

You may ask the church to access or correct your information, withdraw optional birthday sharing, or request deletion where applicable. Contact the Data Protection Officer through the church contact form. The church should respond according to its approved data protection process.

## Kinyarwanda (draft)

### Uburenganzira ku makuru bwite

Iyi nyandiko iracyategurwa kandi igomba gusuzumwa n’Itorero na Ushinzwe Kurinda Amakuru Bwite mbere yo gushyirwa ku rubuga. Itorero riracyemeza uwatanga serivisi z’ububiko bw’amakuru, igihugu cyangwa akarere amakuru azabikwamo, uburyo bwo kuvana cyangwa kohereza amakuru hanze y’u Rwanda, aho Ushinzwe Kurinda Amakuru Bwite azajya abarizwa ku mugaragaro, n’igihe inyandiko z’ibibazo byakemuwe zizabikwa.

### Ushinzwe amakuru

Umugenzuzi w’amakuru ni **Church of the Nazarene–Remera**. Ushinzwe Kurinda Amakuru Bwite ni **Byumvuhore Frederic**. Kugeza igihe itorero rizemeza aderesi ya imeyili cyangwa nimero ya telefoni yo kumuhamagara, ushobora kohereza ikibazo unyuze ku [rupapuro rwo kutwandikira](/contact).

### Amakuru urubuga rushobora kwakira

Hakurikijwe serivisi ukoresha, itorero rishobora kwakira amazina yawe, imeyili, nimero ya telefoni, aho utuye, itariki y’amavuko, igitsina, amakuru y’ubunyamuryango n’itsinda ukoreramo, icyifuzo cyo gukurikiranwa nk’umushyitsi, ibisubizo ku birori, ubutumwa, ibyifuzo by’isengesho, n’amakuru abakozi bandika yerekeye kwitabira amateraniro, ubufasha bwa gishumba cyangwa amaturo. Ijambo ry’ibanga ry’ konti ribikwa rihinduwe mu buryo butaryerekana uko ryakabaye.

### Impamvu dukoresha aya makuru

Dukoresha aya makuru kugira ngo dusubize ubutumwa, twakire abashyitsi, dutange ubufasha bwa gishumba, ducunge konti z’abanyamuryango n’inyandiko z’itorero, dutegure amatsinda n’ibikorwa, kandi twandike amaturo yakiriwe n’abakozi. Urubuga ntirukora ubwishyu kuri interineti.

### Abashobora kuyabona

Amakuru y’ubunyamuryango aboneka ku muyoboke bireba no ku bakozi b’itorero babifitiye uburenganzira. Ibyifuzo by’isengesho bigarukira ku itsinda ry’abashumba ryagenwe. Urubuga rusange ntirugaragaza urutonde rw’abanyamuryango cyangwa urutonde rw’amavuko. Abanyamuryango bafite imyaka 16 cyangwa irenga bashobora kwemera ko izina ryabo rya mbere n’umunsi n’ukwezi bavukiyeho bigaragarira abanyamuryango binjiye muri konti. Bashobora kubihagarika igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Urubuga ntirugaragaza amakuru y’amavuko y’abari munsi y’imyaka 16.

Amakuru y’itorero yemejwe gushyirwa ahagaragara, nk’amakuru y’amatsinda, ibikorwa, inyigisho, amazina n’amafoto, ashobora kugaragara ku rubuga rusange.

### Ububiko n’igihe amakuru azabikwa — biracyategereje kwemezwa

Urubuga rukoresha MongoDB. Mbere yo gushyira iyi nyandiko ahagaragara, itorero rigomba kwemeza izina rya serivisi ikoreshwa mu bubiko bw’amakuru, igihugu cyangwa akarere amakuru azabikwamo, niba amakuru azoherezwa hanze y’u Rwanda n’uburenganzira bukenewe. Itorero riteganya kubika ubutumwa, ibyifuzo by’abashyitsi n’ibyifuzo by’isengesho igihe bigikurikiranwa. Rigomba kwemeza igihe n’uburyo inyandiko zimaze gukemurwa zizahanagurwa cyangwa zikabikwa mu bubiko. Amakuru y’ubunyamuryango, kwitabira, ubufasha n’imari ashobora kugira ibihe bitandukanye byo kubikwa.

### Uburenganzira n’ibibazo

Ushobora gusaba kureba cyangwa gukosora amakuru yawe, guhagarika uburenganzira watanze bwo kugaragaza amavuko, cyangwa gusaba ko amakuru asibwa aho amategeko abyemera. Bwira Ushinzwe Kurinda Amakuru Bwite unyuze ku rupapuro rwo kutwandikira. Itorero rigomba gusubiza hakurikijwe amabwiriza yaryo yemejwe yo kurinda amakuru.
